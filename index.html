Chapter 5Your audience

What we do best: understanding your audience

Before anything gets written, we read what your followers already tell you. The product is built from that, so it answers the questions your audience keeps asking.

  • Which posts people save, because saves show what they want to keep
  • The questions that come up again and again in your comments
  • What followers say they made from your videos, and where they got stuck
  • The words you use, so the product sounds like you

It's also how we tell if it's a fit. If your audience isn't asking for something, we'll say so.

Chapter 7Who it suits

This works best if

  • You have 10,000 followers or more, with no upper limit
  • Your audience is active, not just big: people comment, save your posts and ask the same questions again and again
  • You post regularly and your recent posts still get seen
  • You don't have your own product yet, or it's been "coming soon" for a while

Chapter 8Questions

Questions creators ask first

Who owns the product?

You do. It's sold under your name, on your page. We keep the right to show it in our portfolio only if you say yes.

What if it doesn't sell?

Then neither of us earns, and you haven't paid anything. That's why we only work with accounts where we can see the demand in the comments first.

Where does it sell, and who handles taxes?

On the link-in-bio store you already use, like Beacons or Stan, or on a store we set up for you. Platforms that act as the seller of record handle VAT and sales tax for each purchase.

Will it sound like me?

We write from your captions, videos and comments, and you edit anything that feels off before it goes live. Nothing is published without your approval.

How much of my time does it take?

About two to three hours in total: a 20-minute form, one read-through of the product, and a few posts about it at launch. No calls needed.
